EXPLAIN ANALYZE of A (SELECT only, no workers): about 4.1 s for to_tsvector and expanding the terms,
about 1.4 s for the aggregation by doc_id and its join (about 15%), about 0.9 s for the aggregation by
term, about 1.2 s for the join on term and the weights; total 9.2 s with instrumentation.
Plans: a total in a lateral aggregate or a scalar subquery prevents the parallel plan; a SQL function
total(tsv) in the OFFSET 0 subquery is inlined and makes to_tsvector run twice per document.
Conclusion: not adopted; the saving is within the noise and B/C cost about as much as they save.
